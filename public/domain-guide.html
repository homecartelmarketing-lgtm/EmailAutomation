Why is this step required?

HomeCartel's newly built automated marketing engine sends customer retention emails (abandoned checkout recovery, welcome vouchers, and order care). Major email providers (Gmail, Yahoo, Apple Mail) require cryptographic domain authorization (DKIM, SPF, and DMARC) so emails arrive directly in customer inboxes rather than spam.

Security Guarantee: GoDaddy's official Domain Connect standard securely publishes only the 4 email routing records. It never exposes your GoDaddy login password, payment methods, or existing website hosting settings.
